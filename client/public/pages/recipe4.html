Recipe Name: Chicken Parm with Pink Sauce Pasta

Recipe ID: 2

Course: Main Course

Ingredients

Ingredient ID Name Quantity
201 Penne Pasta 250g
202 Breaded Chicken 150g
203 Tomato & Cream Mix 1.5 cups

Description

Fry the breaded chicken until golden. Mix marinara sauce with heavy cream to create a pink sauce. Toss with cooked penne and top with melted cheese.
