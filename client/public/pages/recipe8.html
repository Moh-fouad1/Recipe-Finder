Recipe Name: Classic New York Cheesecake

Recipe ID: 8

Course: Dessert

Ingredients

Ingredient ID Name Quantity
801 Cream Cheese 500g
802 Graham Crackers 200g
803 Sour Cream 1 cup

Description

Create a crust with crushed crackers and butter. Mix cream cheese, sugar, and sour cream until smooth. Pour onto crust and bake until set.
