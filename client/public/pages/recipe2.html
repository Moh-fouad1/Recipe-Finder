Chicken Parm with Pink Sauce Pasta

Recipe ID: 2 | Course: Main Course

Ingredients

IDNameQuantity
201Penne Pasta250g
202Breaded Chicken150g
203Tomato & Cream Mix1.5 cups

Description

Fry the breaded chicken until golden. Mix marinara sauce with heavy cream to create a pink sauce. Toss with cooked penne and top with melted cheese.
