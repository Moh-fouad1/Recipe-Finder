Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Recipe ID: 1 | Course: Main Course

Ingredients

IDNameQuantity
101Fettuccine200g
102Chicken Breast2 pieces
103Heavy Cream1 cup

Description

Boil the pasta. Sauté sliced chicken in butter until cooked. Add heavy cream and parmesan cheese to create a sauce, then combine with the pasta.
