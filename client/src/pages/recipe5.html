Recipe Name: Spicy Buffalo Wings

Recipe ID: 5

Course: Appetizers

Ingredients

Ingredient ID Name Quantity
501 Chicken Wings 10 pieces
502 Buffalo Sauce 0.5 cups
503 Butter 2 tbsp

Description

Bake or fry wings until crispy. Toss in a mixture of buffalo sauce and melted butter. Serve with celery and blue cheese dressing.
