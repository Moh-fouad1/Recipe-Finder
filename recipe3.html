Recipe Name: Chicken Shawarma

Recipe ID: 3

Course: Main Course

Ingredients

Ingredient ID Name Quantity
301 Marinated Chicken 300g
302 Pita Bread 2 loaves
303 Garlic Sauce 3 tbsp

Description

Grill the marinated chicken until charred and tender. Slice into thin strips and serve in pita bread with garlic sauce and pickles.
