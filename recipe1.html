Recipe Name: Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Recipe ID: 1

Course: Main Course

Ingredients

Ingredient ID Name Quantity
101 Fettuccine 200g
102 Chicken Breast 2 pieces
103 Heavy Cream 1 cup

Description

Boil the pasta. Sauté sliced chicken in butter until cooked. Add heavy cream and parmesan cheese to create a sauce, then combine with the pasta.
