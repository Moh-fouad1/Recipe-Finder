Chicken Shawarma

Recipe ID: 3 | Course: Main Course

Ingredients

IDNameQuantity
301Marinated Chicken300g
302Pita Bread2 loaves
303Garlic Sauce3 tbsp

Description

Grill the marinated chicken until charred and tender. Slice into thin strips and serve in pita bread with garlic sauce and pickles.
