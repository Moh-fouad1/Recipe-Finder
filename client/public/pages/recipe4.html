Crispy Vegetable Spring Rolls

Recipe ID: 4 | Course: Appetizers

Ingredients

IDNameQuantity
401Spring Roll Wrappers5 sheets
402Shredded Cabbage1 cup
403Carrots1 medium

Description

Stir-fry shredded vegetables with soy sauce. Place filling on wrappers, roll tightly, and deep fry until crispy and golden brown.
