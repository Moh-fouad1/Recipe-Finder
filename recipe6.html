Recipe Name: Traditional Beef Lasagna

Recipe ID: 6

Course: Main Course

Ingredients

Ingredient ID Name Quantity
601 Lasagna Sheets 9 pieces
602 Ground Beef 400g
603 Bolognese Sauce 2 cups

Description

Layer pasta sheets with beef bolognese and bechamel sauce. Repeat layers and top with mozzarella. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes.
