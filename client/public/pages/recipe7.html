Rich Chocolate Lava Cake

Recipe ID: 7 | Course: Dessert

Ingredients

Ingredient ID Name Quantity
701Dark Chocolate100g
702Eggs2 large
703Sugar0.25 cups

Description

Melt chocolate and butter together. Whisk eggs with sugar, then fold in the chocolate. Bake for 10-12 minutes so the center remains molten.
